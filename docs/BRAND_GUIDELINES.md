# Brand Guidelines

> **Naming is intentionally not final.** The product is currently called
> **Baybridge** (the repo is `orbit`); treat the name as provisional. Everything
> below is expressed so it survives a rename.
>
> The **canonical mission/vision** belongs in
> [`PRODUCT_VISION.md`](PRODUCT_VISION.md) (awaiting your verbatim content). The
> mission stated here is a *working* articulation derived from what the product
> actually does, to guide voice and design until the canonical text lands — it
> should be reconciled with `PRODUCT_VISION.md`, not compete with it.

## Working mission

Give multi-location practices one trustworthy, live picture of their supplies — and
the calm, obvious next action — so they stop firefighting spreadsheets and stop
wasting money on stock they already have.

## Positioning

- **For:** multi-location practices (dental/ortho first; the architecture allows
  expansion to other industries later).
- **Against:** the status quo of per-office spreadsheets, memory, and re-keying
  orders into distributor sites.
- **Not:** a generic inventory tool bolted onto a practice — it's shaped around how
  a practice actually checks, orders, and moves supplies.

## Voice

- **Plain and calm.** No jargon, no urgency-theater. The product removes stress; the
  writing should too.
- **Specific over generic.** Say exactly what happened or what's wrong
  ("Cabinet 'X' isn't a defined label at any location — left unset"), never a vague
  flag.
- **Respectful of expertise.** The user knows their practice; the product organizes
  and surfaces, it doesn't lecture.
- **Honest.** Show real state — including "no custom logo yet" or a stale count —
  rather than papering over it.

## Tone by context

- **In-app microcopy:** brief, action-oriented, present-tense outcomes.
- **Onboarding:** encouraging, low-friction, never blocking on optional fields.
- **Errors/warnings:** precise and non-blaming; tell the user what to do next.
- **Marketing (future):** confident and concrete — lead with the real problems in
  [`CUSTOMER_PROBLEMS.md`](CUSTOMER_PROBLEMS.md), not adjectives.

## Visual identity (as built)

- **Palette:** Baybridge navy `#14263D` (primary) + teal `#4089A2` (accent) as the
  platform default; each practice can override with its own colors. Semantic status
  colors (green/amber/red) are fixed and never per-practice. See
  [`UI_UX_GUIDELINES.md`](UI_UX_GUIDELINES.md).
- **Mark:** a bridge icon + "BAYBRIDGE" wordmark on signed-out screens; the
  practice's own logo once inside. "Powered by Baybridge" persists.
- **Feel:** clean, uncluttered, status-legible at a glance; the dashboard answers
  "what do I do next?" before it answers anything else.
